Spage2vec is an unsupervised, segmentation-free approach to the spatial transcriptomic diversity of complex tissues, at subcellular resolution. It represents the spatial transcriptomic landscape of a tissue sample as a graph, and uses graph representation learning to create a lower-dimensional representation of local gene expression. These viewers show the spage2vec signatures of four datasets. We thank Mats Nilsson, Sten Linnarsson and Xiaowei Zhuang for making their datasets publicly available.

- [In situ sequencing, mouse brain hippocampal area CA1](https://tissuumaps-demo.serve.scilifelab.se/Spage2vec_ISS_Qian_et_al.tmap). Data: X. Qian et al., _Nature Methods_, 2020. [doi:10.1038/s41592-019-0631-4](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-019-0631-4)
- [osmFISH, mouse brain somatosensory cortex](https://tissuumaps-demo.serve.scilifelab.se/Spage2vec_osmFISH_Codeluppi_et_al.tmap). Data: S. Codeluppi et al., _Nature Methods_, 2018. [doi:10.1038/s41592-018-0175-z](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-018-0175-z)
- [MERFISH, mouse brain hypothalamic preoptic area](https://tissuumaps-demo.serve.scilifelab.se/Spage2vec_MERFISH_Moffitt_et_al.tmap). Data: J. R. Moffitt et al., _Science_, 2018. [doi:10.1126/science.aau5324](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aau5324)
- [MERFISH, human fibroblast cells (IMR90)](https://tissuumaps-demo.serve.scilifelab.se/Spage2vec_MERFISH_Chen_et_al_2015.tmap). Data: K. H. Chen et al., _Science_, 2015. [doi:10.1126/science.aaa6090](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aaa6090)

The spatial gene expression data is available on [Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3897401), and the code to reproduce the results and figures [also on Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.4030404).
