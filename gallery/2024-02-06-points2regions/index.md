Points2Regions is a Python tool, integrated into TissUUmaps and Napari, for clustering spatial biomarkers in omics data. It finds patterns across scales, beyond the limits of traditional clustering methods. It identifies biologically important regions and cell types across various datasets.

Try Points2Regions:

- [Human heart tissue](https://tissuumaps-demo.serve.scilifelab.se/p2r_human_heart.tmap)
- [Mouse brain tissue](https://tissuumaps-demo.serve.scilifelab.se/p2r_mousebrain.tmap)
- [Your own data](https://tissuumaps-demo.serve.scilifelab.se/p2r_own.tmap)
