More and more prostate biopsies, and a worldwide shortage of urological pathologists, strain pathology departments. Grading also varies a lot between and within observers, which can lead to over- and undertreatment. This artificial intelligence system detects, localises and grades prostate cancer with clinically acceptable accuracy. The viewer shows examples of full-resolution digitized biopsies and their AI-based grading.

[Open the overview of all sample datasets](https://sthlm3-2020.serve.scilifelab.se/).

Publication: P. Ström, K. Kartasalo, H. Olsson, L. Solorzano et al. Artificial intelligence for diagnosis and grading of prostate cancer in biopsies: a population-based, diagnostic study. _The Lancet Oncology_, 21(2), 222–232, 2020.
