Targeted cell typing that relies on existing single-cell data gives incomplete and biased maps, which can hide the molecular and cellular diversity of a tissue section. Here, spage2vec, a de novo approach, spatially resolves and characterizes the cellular diversity during human heart development. The viewer shows the data of the in situ sequencing experiment and the identified cell types.

[Open the human heart in TissUUmaps 3](https://tissuumaps-demo.serve.scilifelab.se/human_heart.tmap).

Publication: R. S. Marco Salas, X. Yuan, C. Sylvén, M. Nilsson, C. Wählby and G. Partel. De novo spatiotemporal modelling of cell-type signatures identifies novel cell populations in the developmental human heart. _PLOS Computational Biology_, 2022. [doi:10.1371/journal.pcbi.1010366](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1010366)
