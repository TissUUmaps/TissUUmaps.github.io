The lung contains many specialized cell types: epithelial, endothelial, neuronal, stromal and immune cells. To understand how this diversity develops into a healthy mature lung, this atlas captures the gene expression of all cells of the developing organ, in time and space, during the first trimester.

Viewers:

- Single-cell RNA sequencing: [UMAP](https://hdca-lung.serve.scilifelab.se/UMAP.tmap?path=private/HDCA/UMAP/) of the clusters and sub-clusters, gene expression and metadata.
- In situ sequencing: [pcw 5](https://hdca-lung.serve.scilifelab.se/05.tmap?path=private/HDCA/ISS/), [pcw 6](https://hdca-lung.serve.scilifelab.se/06.tmap?path=private/HDCA/ISS/), [pcw 13](https://hdca-lung.serve.scilifelab.se/13.tmap?path=private/HDCA/ISS/). Spot locations and identities, cell type probabilities per bin and imputed genes.
- SCRINSHOT: [pcw 6](https://hdca-lung.serve.scilifelab.se/pcw6.tmap?path=private/HDCA/SCRINSHOT/), [pcw 8](https://hdca-lung.serve.scilifelab.se/pcw8.tmap?path=private/HDCA/SCRINSHOT/), [pcw 11](https://hdca-lung.serve.scilifelab.se/pcw11.tmap?path=private/HDCA/SCRINSHOT/), [pcw 14](https://hdca-lung.serve.scilifelab.se/pcw14.tmap?path=private/HDCA/SCRINSHOT/). Spot locations and identities.
- Spatial Transcriptomics: [pcw 6](https://hdca-lung.serve.scilifelab.se/Counts_25.tmap?path=private/HDCA/ST/), [pcw 8](https://hdca-lung.serve.scilifelab.se/Counts_29.tmap?path=private/HDCA/ST/), [pcw 10](https://hdca-lung.serve.scilifelab.se/Counts_06.tmap?path=private/HDCA/ST/), [pcw 11](https://hdca-lung.serve.scilifelab.se/Counts_23.tmap?path=private/HDCA/ST/). Gene expression per gene or as pie charts.
