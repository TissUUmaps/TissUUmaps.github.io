# About

TissUUmaps is a community-driven, open-source project. Everyone is welcome to [contribute](https://github.com/TissUUmaps/TissUUmaps).

The [SciLifeLab BioImage Informatics Unit (BIIF)](https://www.scilifelab.se/units/bioimage-informatics/), part of [SciLifeLab](https://www.scilifelab.se/) and [NBIS](https://nbis.se/), is a core contributor to the project.

## History

<Figure
  src="/img/about/hackathon.jpg"
  alt="Participants of the TissUUmaps hackathon"
  caption="TissUUmaps Hackathon, 2021-09-29"
/>

TissUUmaps goes back to 2011. Carolina Wählby, then at the Broad Institute, was contacted by Mats Nilsson at Stockholm University, who had invented a method to sequence mRNA directly in tissue sections and needed computational tools to decode the image data. This led to a joint [publication](https://www.nature.com/articles/nmeth.2563) on in situ sequencing in Nature Methods in 2013, and a collaboration that is still ongoing.

In 2015, TissUUmaps became a research project of the Wählby lab at the [Department of Information Technology](https://www.it.uu.se/), Uppsala University. The first paper describing the tool followed in 2020, and TissUUmaps 3, published in 2023, extended it from in situ sequencing to large-scale spatial omics data in general. Along the way, the project also produced new methods for pre- and post-processing of in situ sequencing data.

Over time, the [SciLifeLab BioImage Informatics Unit (BIIF)](https://www.scilifelab.se/units/bioimage-informatics/), originally founded by Carolina Wählby and now headed by Anna Klemm, took on a growing role in the project. In 2025, the BIIF began an architectural overhaul of the tool, which has since led to today's TissUUmaps 4.

## Funding

TissUUmaps is currently funded by:

- the [SciLifeLab BioImage Informatics Unit (BIIF)](https://www.scilifelab.se/units/bioimage-informatics/), through technology development projects (TDPs);
- the [SciLifeLab and Wallenberg National Program for Data-Driven Life Science (DDLS)](https://www.scilifelab.se/data-driven/), as a [Research Data Community Project (RDCP)](https://www.scilifelab.se/news/four-collaborative-projects-selected-in-the-rdcp-call/).

Previously, it was funded by:

- the European Research Council, through an ERC consolidator grant to Carolina Wählby (ERC CoG 682810);
- the Swedish Foundation for Strategic Research (SSF), through the [SysMic](https://sysmic.ki.se/) project (SB16-0046), with Staffan Strömblad as PI and Carolina Wählby as co-PI, and through the Big Data grant [‘Hierarchical Analysis of Temporal and Spatial Image Data - From intelligent data acquisition via smart data-management to confident predictions’](http://haste.research.it.uu.se/), with Carolina Wählby as PI.
